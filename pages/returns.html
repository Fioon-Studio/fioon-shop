A little time to decide

Contact us within 30 days of delivery to request a return. Items should be unworn, unwashed, free from pet hair and odours, and returned with their original packaging and included accessories.

Start with a hello

Email 1145635941@qq.com with your order number and the item you would like to return. Wait for the return address and instructions before sending a parcel. A return address will be confirmed before sales begin.

Return shipping

For a change of mind or a size choice, the customer covers return shipping. If we send an incorrect or faulty item, we will arrange an appropriate remedy and cover reasonable agreed return costs.

Refunds & exchanges

Our proposed processing time is 5–10 business days after the return is received and inspected; your payment provider may take additional time. Refunds go to the original payment method. Exchanges depend on stock and can be handled as a return and a new order.

Your consumer rights

This proposed policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights applicable to your order. Destination-specific terms will be reviewed before launch.
