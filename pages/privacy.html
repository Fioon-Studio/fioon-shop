Privacy & your choices.

This preview stores your saved products and bag in local storage on your device. It has no customer-account database, advertising trackers or newsletter collection.

Clicking our email link opens your email application; any information you send will be processed by the email providers involved. The hosting provider may also process technical request data.

The live store’s business identity, data controller, payment and shipping providers, retention periods and request process must be added before launch. This page describes the current preview, not a completed policy for a live commerce service.

You can clear saved products and the bag from Privacy & storage on the homepage, or clear this site’s browser storage.

Support chat

A necessary first-party session cookie keeps your conversation together. Chat history and product context are held in server memory for up to one hour of inactivity and disappear when the server restarts. Use Reset conversation to clear them immediately. Do not send payment details or sensitive personal information.

When AI support is configured, your question, recent conversation and relevant public product information are sent to MiniMax to generate a reply. Otherwise the service answers directly from the product catalog and labels this clearly. We do not use chat messages for advertising profiles.
