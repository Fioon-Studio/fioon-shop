Questions people ask before the first order

The full FAQ
Are these photographs?

No. Every frame is AI-generated from your own product photographs. Your product is the reference, so the print, colour and trim stay the same; the model, the pose, the background and the light are synthetic. We will not pass a render off as a photograph of a real shoot, and we do not imitate a named brand or a real person's likeness.

Do I own the images commercially?

Yes, including resale and paid advertising, with no attribution requirement and no expiry. What we cannot transfer is the right to resell the raw files as stock photography, or to train a model on them.

Do I have to disclose that they are AI images?

Treat them as AI content. Rules differ by channel, and some marketplaces and ad platforms restrict or label synthetic media. Tell us where you plan to post and we will say plainly what we can deliver for that channel, including a labelled version.

What if I do not like the result?

Use the revision round that comes with the tier. If it is still not usable after that, email us within seven days of delivery and we re-shoot the batch at no charge or refund the tier. You do not have to justify it twice.

Do I need to send you physical samples?

No. The work is done entirely from your photographs, which is why the price and the turnaround stay where they are.
