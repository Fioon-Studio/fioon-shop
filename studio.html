04 WHAT YOU GET

Five things, in every order.

  • Flat-lay and on-model product photos

    Six flat-lays and two model frames per product, JPG at 2000px in sRGB. Your garment, print, hardware and colour stay the same in every frame, because the reference is your photo and not a guess.

  • Short 9:16 videos

    Ten seconds per product, MP4 at 1080x1920 with captions burned in, cut for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

  • English listing copy

    Title, five bullet points and a description, written for US, UK and EU marketplaces from the specs you give us. No invented claims, no made-up certifications.

  • Ad creative sets

    Statics and video cuts at platform sizes for X and TikTok, built from the same images, so your paid posts and your organic posts match each other.

  • One free test frame

    Before any payment: send one product photo, get one frame back. If the look is wrong, say so and we drop it.

06 HOW IT RUNS

Three days from your photos to something you can publish.

Day 1

Your photos, and the shot list.

Five to ten clear shots of the real product: front, back, and a close-up of the print or hardware. Add the colour and pattern names, the sizes you sell, and whether the piece is for cats or dogs. We send the shot list and the tier that fits back the same day.

Day 2

The photography lands.

Flat-lays, on-model frames, and the free test frame for approval. Everything is generated from your own photos, so the garment stays exactly as it is.

Day 3

The rest, in one download.

The vertical videos, the English listing copy and the ad cuts for X and TikTok, labelled by product and format. The Launch tier runs five working days instead of three.

07 QUESTIONS

Before the first order.

How many revisions do I get?

One round on Starter and Growth, two on Launch. A round is one consolidated list of changes to the delivered files, and we return the corrected package within one working day. Any further round is agreed with you before it starts.

Do I own the images commercially?

Yes. Full commercial rights on everything you pay for, including resale, paid advertising, marketplaces and your own site. No attribution, no usage fee, no expiry date. Two things stay with us: the right to resell the raw files as stock photography, and the right to train a model on them. The animals and people in the frames are synthetic, so no real person's likeness is used.

Do I have to disclose that these are AI images?

Treat them as AI content. Rules differ by platform: some marketplaces allow AI-assisted product photography, some restrict it, and several ad platforms ask for a disclosure label. Tell us where it will run and we will say plainly what we can and cannot produce for that channel, including a visible AI label if you need one.

What if I am not happy with the result?

Use the revision round that comes with the tier first. If the work still is not usable after that, email us within seven days of delivery and we re-shoot the batch at no charge or refund the tier in full.

Do I need to send you physical samples?

No. The work is done from your photos, which is exactly why the price and the 48-hour photography turnaround stay where they are. You only need to ship something if you want a real physical shoot, and that is a separate job, quoted per project.
