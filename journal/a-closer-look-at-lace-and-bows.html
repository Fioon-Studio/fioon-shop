A closer look at lace, folds and little bows

By FiOON · Design & everyday life

A small accessory can change the feeling of a familiar portrait. A gathered edge catches the light. A bow adds a focal point. A quiet floral print can look quite different on a curious cat and an equally curious dog. Our design notes are a way to look more closely at those details, without dressing them up in grand claims.

Shape first, then the finishing detail

Gathered collars have a different silhouette from flatter bibs and crossover bandanas. The folds open around the neck when worn; on a tabletop, they relax into another shape. That is why each product has both a dimensional studio view and a separate flat view. They answer different questions.

The first helps you imagine the overall shape. The second lets you look at the back opening, the ends of a ribbon or the position of a fastening. Neither is a substitute for the product’s own size chart.

Let one detail lead

On a floral design, the print may be the first thing you notice. On another, it is a contrasting bow or a scalloped edge. Consider what you enjoy seeing against your companion’s coat, rather than treating one colour combination as a rule. The same design is shown on both a cat and a dog so you can explore that difference.

Decorative details, honestly described

Some designs have pearl-like beads, blue accents or silver-toned ornaments. These descriptions refer to their appearance, not certified gemstones or precious-metal content. Likewise, a collection name is a mood, not a statement about fibre composition or where an item was made.
