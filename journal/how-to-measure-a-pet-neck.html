How to measure your cat or dog for a decorative collar

By FiOON · Design & everyday life

A beautiful accessory only belongs in your pet’s wardrobe if the fit works for them. Start with a real neck measurement, not a breed name, a photograph or a familiar size label. An XS in one design can cover a different range from an XS in another.

Start with a calm moment

Choose a moment when your companion is relaxed. Use a soft measuring tape around the part of the neck where the accessory will sit. Keep the tape level and close to the coat without pulling it tight. If your pet moves away or seems uncomfortable, pause and try another time.

Have someone help you read the tape if necessary. Measure again before writing the number down; a moving pet or a twisted tape can change the result. A photograph is helpful for explaining a question, but it cannot replace the measurement.

Read the chart for that exact design

Open the product page and compare the number with its listed neck ranges. FiOON product pages display inches first, with an in / cm switch. One inch equals 2.54 centimetres. Switching units changes the display, not the physical size of the accessory.

Where two size ranges share an endpoint, or your measurement falls between options, ask us before choosing. Some designs still need their neck ranges confirmed. We leave those measurements unconfirmed rather than substituting a weight or breed estimate.

Check the closure as well as the number

A lace tie, an extension chain, an elastic drawcord and a snap fastening do not adjust in the same way. The open product photograph helps you see the construction. Do not assume that a long decorative ribbon means an unlimited neck range.

A final check before wearing

These are decorative accessories, not walking collars or restraint equipment. Never attach a leash. Supervise wear and remove the accessory if your pet seems uncomfortable. For a fitting question, send the product name and your measured neck circumference to our team.
